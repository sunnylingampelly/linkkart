## 🎯 Design Highlights

### Typography:
- **Headings**: 56px Playfair Display, 800 weight
- **Subheadings**: 32px Playfair Display, 800 weight
- **Body**: 16-22px Inter, 500-700 weight
- **Buttons**: 15-18px Inter, 700 weight, uppercase

### Spacing:
- **Section Padding**: 100-140px
- **Card Gaps**: 48px
- **Card Padding**: 32-56px
- **Generous white space** throughout

### Colors in Action:
- **Headers**: Blue gradient (#5B6CFF → #7B8AFF)
- **Cards**: White (#FFFFFF) with blue borders
- **Hover**: Blue (#5B6CFF) borders and shadows
- **Prices**: Blue-green gradient
- **Buttons**: Blue gradient with hover effects
- **Overlays**: Blue-green gradient (90% opacity)
- **Footer**: Blue gradient with colorful top border

### Animations:
- **Card Hover**: translateY(-12px), 0.4s
- **Avatar Hover**: scale(1.12), translateY(-6px)
- **Image Hover**: scale(1.1)
- **Button Hover**: translateY(-2px)
- **All**: cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)

### Shadows:
- **Cards**: rgba(91, 108, 255, 0.08)
- **Hover**: rgba(91, 108, 255, 0.2-0.5)
- **Buttons**: rgba(91, 108, 255, 0.3-0.4)
- **Colored shadows** match primary blue
